All **1,920 delayed timed iterations** had pending work at finish entry. All
**3,840 timed retirement iterations** passed the harness assertions for actual
address alignment, exact consumer endpoints, no premature release, zero charge
at finish and zero owner references after drain. Access checks full bytes on the
first warmup per case, not every timed access. The raw records reproduce the
stored retirement medians and Claude's aggregation. Individual process values,
all retirement-condition summaries, origin records and artifact hashes are in
[committed evidence](evidence/issue159_pr246_fields_20261028.json).

These results reproduce the Perlmutter findings qualitatively: small aligned
accessor overhead and host blocking for retained unaligned copies. They do not
establish performance equivalence or whole-application throughput loss. Do not
convert a field-call microbenchmark into an event-rate regression; the number
of accesses, concurrency and overlap differ. The accepted batched input/gather
screen remains valid, scoped to its no-user-kernel/no-calibration workload.

## Measurements

- Existing `bench_field_views.py`: uint64 scalar and 4 MiB array, first and
  cached access; aligned A/B and unaligned B only. Twenty warmups and 200
  measurements per case, four balanced AB/BA fresh-process pairs. Report host
  access latency, CUDA stream elapsed (including host submission gaps), retained
  allocation bytes, and separately isolated unaligned byte-copy duration.
- New `bench_field_retirement.py`: the same shapes/alignment matrix with blocking
  and deferred input-window retirement, representing bulk-off/on ownership policy.
  Materialization completes before timing, then a warmed kernel consumes the
  cached view on another stream with either zero or 10,000,000 clock-tick delay.
  Forty measurements after five warmups per case, four balanced process pairs.
  Measure real `EventPool.finish_retire_next()` host duration and the subsequent
  owner drain separately. Record CUDA consumer span, pending status at entry,
  completion and owner-release status at return, and retained bytes before
  retirement. Delay ticks are a stimulus, not an assumed wall-clock duration.
- Before measurement, two iterations per retirement case exercise both arms.
  Every iteration checks actual typed-address alignment, first/last values read
  by the consumer, zero remaining budget charge and owner references after drain,
  and no owner release before the consumer event completes. Delayed cases must
  exercise at least one pending consumer. Allocation and kernel launch warmup
  prevent lazy initialization from consuming the intended delay.

Synthetic host locator rows exclude parser/locator-transfer cost. Retirement
uses a hand-built occupied event slot and the real begin/finish methods; it does
not submit the bulk reader or stage disk data. Bulk labels describe the window
retirement policy only. This is a focused API/lifetime microbenchmark, not an
end-to-end cold throughput or IPC-sharing benchmark. The cold policy does not
apply to this in-memory measurement.

Unaligned A is intentionally excluded because its typed view is unsafe. Do not
infer the unaligned retirement cost from the safe-data dense cold screen.
Cross-process summaries report means and sample SD of per-process medians;
within-process p10/median/p90 remain available. There are no pytest performance
thresholds. The measured costs are accepted for this stage under the disposition below.
